My name is Roi Mor. After years in Israeli high-tech and startups, a personal crisis during the Covid period led me on a journey of deep change. I understood that true happiness isn't found in possessions, but in genuine connection with yourself and those around you.

I live my path -- not just teach it. I believe in combining lightness within the spiritual journey, alongside a deep understanding that this isn't really a path that ends, and yes it's scary, but in my eyes, fear is an incredible indication of personal development potential.

I'm also one of the founders of the wellness center [Hormesis](https://www.hormesis.club), where I guide groups and lead various business processes.
